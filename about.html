About North Star Bakery

Our Story

North Star Bakery was created to bring fresh, handmade baked goods to our neighborhood. Our goal is to create a welcoming bakery where local customers, commuters, families, and event planners can find something made with care.

Our Sourcing Philosophy

We believe great baked goods begin with quality ingredients. We focus on carefully selected ingredients and prepare our products in small batches whenever possible.

Meet Our Team

Our bakers and staff share a passion for good food and friendly service. From preparing bread in the morning to helping customers plan special orders, our team works together to serve the community.
